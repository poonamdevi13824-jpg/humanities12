NCERT Humanities Class 12 • Sociology

Indian Society

Core idea: Sociology helps us understand social structures, inequality, institutions, and social change in India.

1. Social Institutions

Social institutions like family, education, religion, and caste organise social life. They define roles, expectations, and forms of cooperation.

  • Family provides social identity and support.
  • Education shapes values and mobility.
  • Religion and caste influence social belonging.

2. Change and Inequality

Indian society has undergone rapid change, but inequality still remains in economic, social, and educational spheres. Understanding this helps explain everyday experiences.

  • Urbanisation and modernisation affect cultural values.
  • Access to resources remains unequal.
  • Social reform can reduce exclusion and discrimination.

3. Important Points

  • Social institutions and their function
  • Structure of inequality
  • Social change and modernisation
