Politics in India since Independence

Core idea: Indian democracy evolved through challenges of partition, constitution-making, and social diversity.

1. Formation of a Democratic State

India adopted a democratic Constitution and tried to build a system based on justice, liberty, equality, and fraternity. This was a major step in nation-building.

  • Constitution established institutions and rights.
  • Parliamentary democracy was adopted after independence.
  • Political equality became a guiding principle.

2. Major Challenges

India faced poverty, economic restructuring, linguistic diversity, and social inequality. These affected the democratic process and policy-making.

  • Regional differences shaped political demands.
  • National unity required balancing diversity and development.
  • Leadership and institutional reform influenced outcomes.

3. Key Revision Points

  • Constitution and rights
  • Democratic institutions
  • Challenges to political stability
