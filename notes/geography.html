NCERT Humanities Class 12 • Geography

Fundamentals of Human Geography

Core idea: Human geography studies how people interact with land, resources, and space in different regions.

1. Human Geography and Environment

Human geography focuses on the relationship between human beings and the physical environment. People adapt to and modify nature according to their needs.

  • Environment shapes settlement and occupation patterns.
  • Technology modifies the way land is used.
  • Economic activity depends on available resources and climate.

2. Population and Distribution

Population studies help explain density, migration, and urbanisation. Distribution is uneven because of physical, economic, and social conditions.

  • Fertile land and water support higher density.
  • Urban centres attract migrants due to jobs and services.
  • Migration changes both origin and destination regions.

3. Revision Focus

  • Resource distribution
  • Population patterns
  • Human dependence on environment

Exam tip: Use examples such as river valleys, coastal regions, and industrial belts for better explanations.
