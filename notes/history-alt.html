Rise of Nationalism in Europe

Core idea: Nationalism shaped modern European states through identity, language, culture, and political movements.

1. Idea of the Nation

The concept of the nation grew from feelings of shared history, language, and culture. People began to identify with a political community rather than a ruler.

  • Nation-states emerged from shared identity.
  • Nationalism challenged dynastic and feudal systems.
  • Cultural symbols helped create belonging.

2. The Revolutionary Spirit

Political revolutions inspired popular participation and challenged old order. They encouraged demands for rights and representation.

  • Revolutions accelerated change in political institutions.
  • Citizenship and political inclusion became central values.
  • States were reshaped around public participation.

3. Key Revision Points

  • Nationalism and political identity
  • Role of revolutions
  • Formation of modern states

Exam tip: Compare ideas of nationalism with the realities of state-building and political reform.
