Introductory Macro Economics

Core idea: Macro economics studies the overall economy: income, output, employment, and inflation.

1. National Income

National income measures the total value of final goods and services produced in an economy over a period of time. It helps in understanding economic performance.

  • GDP and GNP are major indicators.
  • Income flows explain production and distribution.
  • Economic growth is linked to output and consumption.

2. Employment and Inflation

Employment levels and inflation are major indicators of a healthy economy. High unemployment and rising prices create serious pressure on households.

  • Employment depends on investment and industrial output.
  • Inflation reduces purchasing power.
  • Governments use policies to control instability.
