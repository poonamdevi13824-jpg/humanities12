NCERT Humanities Class 12 • History

India and the Contemporary World – II

Core idea: History helps us understand how political, social, and economic changes shaped modern India and the world.

1. Nationalism and Modernisation

Nationalism emerged as a force that united people around identity, language, culture, and political freedom. It influenced the making of nations and reshaped political boundaries.

  • People began to see nationhood as a shared political identity.
  • Ideas of equality, citizenship, and representation became important.
  • National movements often challenged colonial control and authoritarian rule.

2. Colonialism and Change

Colonial rule changed land use, taxation, labor systems, education, and political administration. These changes created new conflicts and social movements.

  • Large-scale exploitation shaped rural and urban life.
  • Trade and industries expanded under imperial control.
  • New political awareness grew among educated Indians.

3. Important Themes for Revision

  • Impact of nationalism on freedom struggles
  • Role of leaders, movements, and reforms
  • How economic and social transformation shaped society
